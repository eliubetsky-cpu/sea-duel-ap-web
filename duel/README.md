# Sea Duel — Tactical Duel v0.2

Isolated 1v1 experiment. Two identical Brigs:100 HP,20 damage,320 movement,1100 range.
Five hits sink a ship. No crew, boarding, retaliation, AP or damage scaling.
One shot per turn; movement can come before and after firing.
Rocks stop ships and cannonballs. A direct hull hit is required.

Draw your route to sail, preserving its corners. Pull back from your ship to aim automatically.
The original close-up aim camera opens during the pull. The yellow arrow swings in a45-degree sector.
Release to fire exactly along the displayed arrow; return the finger to the start to cancel.
There are no SAIL/AIM/FIRE buttons. END TURN and MENU remain.
The player has no additional random spread. The bot has seeded error up to5.5 degrees.
The bot seeks firing positions and cover after shooting.

This experiment lives at /duel/. The fleet v0.12 root player is preserved.
Source branch:experiment/tactical-duel. Generated public player files only.
